The Italian exam material — the `italian_text` of each statement, its official
`answer`, and the raster figures in `public_html/domandeb_images/` taken from the
Ministry's publication — is the **publicly published question bank of the Italian
Ministero delle Infrastrutture e dei Trasporti**. Under Italian copyright law
(L. 633/1941, art. 5) the official acts of the State are not subject to

## Third-party figures — Wikimedia Commons vectorizations

About half of the files in `public_html/domandeb_images/` are SVG vectorizations
of the official sign designs downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, used in place of
the Ministry's low-resolution rasters. The sign *designs* are official acts and
public domain; the *vectorizations* belong to their authors and carry the licence
each author chose, public domain for most and **CC BY-SA 3.0** or **4.0** for the
rest. One illustration in `public_html/review_media/` (`disco-orario.svg`) is

pages link to. A fork must keep those attributions.
