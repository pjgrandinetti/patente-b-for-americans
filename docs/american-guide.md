# Getting an Italian Driver's License (Patente B) as an American

> **Background, not the reference.** The app's own "How you get an Italian
> license" panel is the authority on the process and is kept current with it.
> This file holds what the app deliberately leaves out: why an American has
> to sit the exam at all, and how to prepare the language. Where the two ever
> disagree, the app is right. Corrected against it on 2026-09-16, when this

## The key fact: no conversion agreement

Unlike many countries, there is **no reciprocal licence conversion agreement between the United States and Italy**. You cannot simply exchange your US licence. The full Patente B process — theory examination and practical examination — is required, regardless of how long you have been driving.

If you are an Italian tax resident, be aware that after **one year of established residency**, driving on a US licence alone becomes illegal and standard insurance cover may lapse. Confirm your residency registration (*iscrizione all'Anagrafe*) date early.

---

## The theory examination

The Italian theory exam is **30 true/false questions in 20 minutes**, drawn from an official bank of just over 7,000. You may get **no more than 3 wrong**. No partial credit. You have **6 months and 2 attempts**, at least a month apart.

Because the question bank is fixed and public, the most effective preparation strategy is straightforward: **work through the entire bank until you know it**. Pattern recognition develops quickly — the questions are formulaic and reuse a limited vocabulary.

### Study resources

- **This app.** It carries the full official bank with an English translation and an explanation for every statement, which is what the Italian-only apps do not offer.
- **Video explanations in Italian.** Searching "patente B teoria" finds walkthroughs of signs, junctions, right of way, speed limits and towing. They double as listening practice.
- The exam is conducted entirely in Italian. **A2 to B1** Italian is enough, since the vocabulary is narrow and comes quickly through drilling.

### Registration

You apply on the **TT2112 form**, filed with your **provincial Motorizzazione** office, with a valid ID, your codice fiscale or health card, the medical certificate, the two pre-printed postal payment slips, and a passport photo. You do not need to be enrolled in a driving school: a *privatista* files the paperwork themselves. The exam itself is held in computerized rooms at the Motorizzazione, never at the driving school.

If a school is enrolling you, it books your exam date, and most will not put you forward until they have seen a good average on **their own** practice sheets, sat in the school's own application. Studying elsewhere is invisible to them, so leave time at the end for their sheets, and ask early what average the school wants, over how many sheets, and how far ahead its exam dates are running. This is the school's own condition rather than a ministry rule.

### After passing: the *foglio rosa*

Upon passing the theory exam, you receive a **foglio rosa** (provisional licence), **valid 12 months**, with up to **3 attempts** at the practical exam inside it. The practical can be taken from **1 month** after the foglio rosa is issued.

## The practical examination

### Mandatory instruction hours

You must complete **6 hours of certified instruction** at an accredited *autoscuola* (D.M. 20 April 2012), in three blocks of **2 hours**: on the autostrada or a main extra-urban road, on a secondary extra-urban road or an urban arterial road, and at night. The driving test can be booked only once all 6 are certified. D.M. 294 of 17 November 2025 raises this to 8 hours in four modules for a foglio rosa issued once its implementing decree takes effect; a foglio rosa issued on 30 October 2026 still took 6. This is the primary cost item. Only once the school certifies them may you practice with an accompanying driver. The companion must sit beside you and supervise, must be **no older than 65**, and must have held a B or higher licence for at least **10 years** (Code art. 122 c. 2). The car must display a **"P"** plate (art. 122 c. 4). Once your first 12 months of residency have passed, your US license no longer lets you drive in Italy at all, so this supervised practice is the only driving you may do until you pass.

### The exam itself

The practical test has three parts: a check that you can **prepare the car** for safe driving (seat, mirrors, belts), **two maneuvers**, one of them in reverse, and **at least 25 minutes in normal traffic**. The examiner judges driving behavior as well as skill. It is run by a Motorizzazione examiner.

Unlike in the US, you do not bring your own car. The test must be taken in a vehicle with **dual controls** (Code art. 121 c. 9), meaning a second brake pedal for the instructor, so in practice it is the driving school's car. A private car qualifies only if approved dual controls are fitted, which almost nobody does.

### Automatic transmission (Codice 78)

You may take the practical exam in an **automatic vehicle**. This results in a "Codice 78 Patente B," which formally restricts the holder to automatic-transmission vehicles. If you drive automatic and have no need to drive manual, this is a practical simplification worth considering — it does not affect day-to-day driving in most circumstances.

### After passing

A **temporary permit** is issued immediately upon passing. The official *patente di guida* card arrives by post within 1–2 months and is valid for driving throughout the **European Union**.

## Language preparation

If your Italian is below A2 when you start, consider:

- **Pimsleur** — audio-based, good for spoken fluency during commutes or walks.
- **italki** — one-on-one sessions with a native tutor; sessions can be focused on practical vocabulary.
- **Dante Alighieri Society** — structured courses, available online and in-person at branches worldwide.

Driving-specific Italian is narrow in scope and learned quickly through question bank drilling, even at A2 level.

## Rough process overview

| Step | What happens |
|------|-------------|
| Obtain medical certificate | Two visits, in order: your own doctor (*medico di base*) writes the *certificato anamnestico* (about €50, not covered by the national health service), then an authorized doctor (*medico autorizzato*) checks your fitness and eyesight and issues the certificate. Through a driving school you enroll first and book the second visit through the school; a *privatista* books both. Required before registering for the theory exam. |
| Register for theory exam | TT2112 form to the provincial Motorizzazione |
| Pass theory exam | 30 questions in 20 min, 3 wrong allowed; *foglio rosa* issued, valid 12 months |
| Complete 8 mandatory driving hours | At an accredited *autoscuola*, in four modules |
| Pass practical exam | Car check, two maneuvers, 25+ min in traffic; temporary permit issued immediately |
| Receive official licence | Arrives by post within 1–2 months; valid across the EU |
