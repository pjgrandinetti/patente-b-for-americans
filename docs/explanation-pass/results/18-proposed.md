**Proposed:** True. A road feature counts as a structural cause of accidents only when it is a defect of the road. **Now:** True. If you cannot move off in first, use a higher gear to reduce wheelspin.

## 24790 (FALSO)

## 24824 (FALSO)

**Proposed:** False. A wheelie lifts the front wheel off the ground, so the vehicle rides on the rear wheel alone and cannot be steered. **Proposta:** Falso. Un'impennata solleva la ruota anteriore da terra, quindi il veicolo poggia sulla sola ruota posteriore e non può essere sterzato.
