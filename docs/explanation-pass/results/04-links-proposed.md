## 19516 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta il transito agli autocarri*

The sign shown bans trucks.

**Link added:** True. No truck symbol is needed: the blank disc closes the road to every vehicle, from bicycles to trucks. [The specific truck-ban sign] exists for roads where only heavy vehicles are excluded.

## 19520 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è un DIVIETO DI TRANSITO*

The sign shown is a ROAD CLOSED sign (DIVIETO DI TRANSITO — closed to all vehicles in both directions).

**Link added:** True. The plain red-bordered ring with no symbol is the ROAD CLOSED sign (divieto di transito): closed to all vehicles in both directions. Do not confuse it with [the red disc with a white bar, which is DO NOT ENTER (senso vietato)].

## 19525 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che la circolazione è a senso unico*

The sign shown indicates that traffic is one-way.

**Link added:** False. One-way traffic is signed by [the blue one-way arrow] at the entrance and [DO NOT ENTER (red disc, white bar)] at the other end. This plain red ring instead closes the road to all vehicles in both directions.

## 19529 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta di entrare in una strada accessibile invece dall'altra parte*

The sign shown prohibits entering a road that can instead be entered from its other end.

**Link added:** True. This is the back side of a one-way street: traffic enters from the opposite end and drives toward you, so from this side you may not enter. The entrance at the other end carries [the blue one-way arrow].

## 19534 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto su una strada a senso unico*

The sign shown is placed on a one-way street.

**Link added:** True. DO NOT ENTER is the reverse side of a one-way street: it guards the end where traffic flows out toward you, while the entrance at the other end carries [the blue one-way arrow].

## 19541 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta il sorpasso*

**Link added:** False. [The no-passing sign shows two cars side by side in a red-bordered ring]; this solid red disc with a white bar bans entry, not the overtaking maneuver.

## 19543 (FALSO)

**Link added:** False. NO ENTRY applies around the clock, every day, and must be observed at night too, because the oncoming traffic it guards against exists at every hour. The 8:00-20:00 window in town belongs to a different sign, [the no-parking sign]; borrowing that window here is the trap. Read the whole sentence: strike «nei centri abitati, dalle ore 8.00 alle ore 20.00» and what is left is true; the added words are what make the statement false.

## 19544 (FALSO)

## 19635 (VERO)

*In presenza del segnale raffigurato è vietato agli autocarri con massa a pieno carico superiore a 3,5 t di sorpassare un motociclo*

Where this sign is posted, it is prohibited for trucks with a maximum laden mass exceeding 3.5 metric tons to overtake a motorcycle.

**Link added:** True. The truck version has no two-wheeler exception, unlike [the general no-passing sign]: a goods vehicle over 3.5 metric tons (7,700 lb) may not overtake anything motorized, a motorcycle least of all. Untouched by the sign, and still free to pass, are cars, goods vehicles at or under that mass, and passenger-transport vehicles at any weight, buses, articulated buses and motorhomes.

## 19638 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato consente ad un autocarro di massa complessiva superiore a 3,5 tonnellate di sorpassare un motociclo*

With this sign, a truck with a total mass exceeding 3.5 metric tons may overtake a motorcycle.

**Link added:** False. The truck sign copies [the two-car picture] but not the two-wheeler exception: over 3.5 metric tons (7,700 lb), a goods vehicle may pass nothing motorized, and a motorcycle is motorized. The free list is unchanged: cars, goods vehicles at or under 3.5 metric tons, and passenger-transport vehicles at any weight, buses, articulated buses and motorhomes.

## 19558 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato consente il transito agli autotreni, autoarticolati ed autosnodati*

With this sign, truck-and-trailer combinations (autotreni), tractor-trailers (autoarticolati), and articulated buses (autosnodati) may proceed.

**Link added:** True. The Code keeps two ideas apart: transito is being on the road at all, sorpasso is passing another vehicle. This sign restricts only the second, so it forbids overtaking without closing the road to any category. The point is not empty: [a separate prohibition sign, showing a truck-and-trailer and a tractor-trailer], does ban these very combinations from the road, and mistaking one sign for the other is the trap. So truck-and-trailer combinations, tractor-trailers and articulated buses keep driving on this road; what they may not do here is pass another motor vehicle.

## 19559 (VERO)

*In presenza del segnale raffigurato è vietato sorpassare anche gli autoveicoli di massa complessiva inferiore a 3,5 tonnellate*

Where this sign is posted, it is prohibited to overtake even motor vehicles with total mass less than 3.5 metric tons.

**Link added:** True. The general no-passing sign sets no weight threshold: the mass of the vehicle passed and of the one passing are both irrelevant, so a light car is as protected as a heavy truck. The banned group is every motor vehicle except two-wheelers: cars, trucks, buses, agricultural machines, work machines, electric or very slow vehicles. Only two-wheeled mopeds and motorcycles and non-motorized vehicles may be passed, and the 3.5 metric ton (7,700 lb) figure belongs to [the other sign, the one that binds goods vehicles above that mass].

## 19607 (VERO)

**Link added:** True. It fixes the maximum: any speed at or below the figure is lawful. The trap is reading it as a minimum; [the minimum-speed sign is a different, blue one], and this red-bordered disc only caps your speed.

## 19610 (VERO)
