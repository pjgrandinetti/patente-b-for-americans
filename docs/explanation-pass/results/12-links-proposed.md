## 23614 (VERO)

*Se si parcheggia in una zona regolamentata con parchimetro, occorre esporre l'apposito tagliando, in modo che sia ben visibile*

If parking in a regulated zone with parking meter, you must display the appropriate ticket so that it is clearly visible.

**Link added:** True. The displayed ticket is the proof that the paid time is running, and it works only if enforcement can read it from outside the vehicle. The same logic governs [the parking disc], which must show the arrival time.

## 23615 (VERO)

*Per parcheggiare in una zona regolamentata mediante disco orario, bisogna esporre in modo ben visibile l'orario di arrivo*

To park in a regulated zone using a parking disc, you must display the arrival time clearly visible.

**Link added:** True. [The disc] shows your arrival time, set by hand on the dial and displayed face out on the dashboard; the warden reads it to work out when your period ends.

## 23619 (FALSO)

*In una zona in cui la sosta è regolamentata mediante disco orario, prima che scada il tempo di validità della sosta è consentito aggiornare l'orario, senza che il veicolo venga spostato*

In a zone where parking is regulated by parking disc, before the validity time of parking expires it is permitted to update the time without moving the vehicle.

**Link added:** False. With [a parking disc] you show the time you arrived, and the free period runs from it. To start a new period you must move the vehicle; turning the dial forward while parked is not allowed. Note that a few cities sell parking through a phone app that can be topped up remotely, but that is a different system: a question about the disc is asking about the disc.

## 23679 (VERO)

*La fermata è vietata in corrispondenza dei segnali orizzontali di preselezione e lungo le corsie di canalizzazione*

Stopping is prohibited at lane-marking signs and along canalisation lanes.

**Link added:** True. [Lane-selection markings] and channelizing lanes are among the places where the fermata itself is banned, and a fermata ban always brings the sosta ban with it.

## 23688 (VERO)

*La sosta e la fermata sono sempre vietate sugli attraversamenti pedonali*

Parking and stopping are always prohibited on pedestrian crossings.

**Link added:** True. A vehicle standing [on the stripes] hides the pedestrians and forces them to step out into the traffic to get around it, so the ban has no exceptions: not even the brief halt to drop off a passenger is allowed there.

## 23741 (VERO)

*La sosta e la fermata sono vietate in corrispondenza dei segnali orizzontali di preselezione e lungo le corsie di canalizzazione*

Parking and stopping are forbidden at horizontal lane selection signs and along channeling lanes.

**Link added:** True. [Lane-selection markings] and channelizing lanes are among the places where even the brief fermata is banned, so the prolonged sosta is banned there as a matter of course.

## 23745 (FALSO)

*La sosta è vietata in corrispondenza dei segnali orizzontali di preselezione ma non lungo le corsie di canalizzazione*

Parking is forbidden at horizontal lane selection signs but not along channeling lanes.

**Link added:** False. The ban covers both places: sosta, and even fermata, is forbidden at [lane-selection markings] and along channelizing lanes alike. **Link added:** False. Pedestrian crossings are on the always-forbidden list: both sosta and fermata are banned there without exception, because [a vehicle on the stripes] hides pedestrians and pushes them out into traffic. ## 23754 (VERO)

*La sosta è vietata negli spazi riservati allo stazionamento e alla fermata degli autobus, dei filobus e dei veicoli circolanti su rotaia*

Parking is prohibited in spaces reserved for stopping and standing of buses, trolleybuses and rail vehicles.

**Link added:** True. [The reserved space] exists so buses, trolleybuses and rail vehicles can pull in; parking there is banned and is one of the violations that also cost license points.

## 23755 (VERO)

*La sosta è vietata a meno di 15 metri dal segnale di fermata di autobus, filobus e veicoli circolanti su rotaia, qualora gli spazi di stazionamento non siano delimitati*

Parking is prohibited less than 15 meters from the bus, trolleybus and rail vehicle stop sign, if parking spaces are not delimited.

**Link added:** True. Where no space is marked, the protection becomes a distance: no sosta within 15 m (16 yd) of the stop sign. [Where the space is marked], the reserved area itself is off limits.

## 23758 (FALSO)

*La sosta è consentita negli spazi riservati allo stazionamento e alla fermata degli autobus, dei filobus e dei veicoli circolanti su rotaia purché sia di breve durata*

Parking is permitted in spaces reserved for stopping and standing of buses, trolleybuses and rail vehicles provided it is of short duration.

**Link added:** False. [The reserved bus and rail spaces] are off limits to the sosta regardless of duration, and the violation also costs license points; brevity never lifts a sosta ban.

## 23762 (VERO)

*La sosta è vietata negli spazi riservati alla fermata o alla sosta dei veicoli per persone invalide*

Parking is prohibited in spaces reserved for stopping or parking of vehicles for disabled persons.

**Link added:** True. [Disabled parking spaces] are strictly protected: the sosta is banned there and the violation is one of those that also cost license points.

## 23766 (FALSO)

*La sosta è consentita negli spazi riservati alla fermata o alla sosta dei veicoli per persone invalide purché di breve durata*

Parking is permitted in spaces reserved for stopping or parking of vehicles for disabled persons provided it is of short duration.

**Link added:** False. The sosta in [disabled spaces] is banned regardless of duration, and it also costs license points; brevity never lifts a sosta ban.

## 23780 (VERO)

*La sosta negli spazi riservati allo stazionamento e alla fermata di autobus, filobus e veicoli circolanti su rotaia comporta, tra l'altro, la sottrazione di punti dalla patente.*

Parking in spaces reserved for stopping and standing of buses, trolleybuses and rail vehicles results, among other things, in the deduction of points from the driving license.

**Link added:** True. [Bus, trolleybus and rail spaces] are among the parking violations that carry license points as well as a fine, together with unmarked bus stops (15 m), disabled spaces and ramps, and reserved lanes.

## 23782 (VERO)

*La sosta negli spazi riservati alla fermata e alla sosta dei veicoli per persone invalide, comporta, tra l'altro, la sottrazione di punti dalla patente*

Parking in spaces reserved for stopping and parking of vehicles for disabled persons results, among other things, in the deduction of points from the driving license.

**Link added:** True. [Disabled spaces] are among the few parking violations that cost license points on top of the fine, together with bus stops, curb ramps and reserved lanes.

## 23785 (FALSO)

*La sosta negli spazi riservati allo stazionamento e alla fermata di autobus, filobus e veicoli circolanti su rotaia, è consentita se si utilizza il disco orario*

Parking in spaces reserved for stopping and standing of buses, trolleybuses and rail vehicles is permitted if a parking disc is used.

**Link added:** False. Parking in [spaces reserved for buses, trolleybuses, and rail vehicles] is prohibited and results in loss of license points; using [a parking disc] does not make it lawful.

## 23787 (FALSO)

*La sosta negli spazi riservati alla fermata e alla sosta dei veicoli per persone invalide comporta una sanzione pecuniaria, ma non la sottrazione di punti dalla patente del conducente*

Parking in spaces reserved for stopping and parking of vehicles for disabled persons results in a pecuniary fine, but not in the deduction of points from the driver's license.

**Link added:** False. Parking in [disabled spaces] costs both a fine and license points. Read the whole sentence: strike «ma non la sottrazione di punti dalla patente del conducente» and what is left is true; the added words are what make the statement false.
