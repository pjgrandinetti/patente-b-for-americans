# Link only, wording unchanged (31)

## 21812 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preavvisa la distanza dal confine con uno Stato che fa parte dell'Unione Europea*

The sign shown announces the upcoming distance from a border with a State that is part of the European Union.

**Link added:** True. This is the advance version of the EU border sign: the ring of EU stars names the country ahead, and the added figure (1 km) is the distance to the border. [The plain version without a distance] stands at the border itself.

## 21813 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato non obbliga ad arrestarsi per il controllo doganale al confine di Stato*

The sign shown does not require stopping for customs inspection at the State border.

**Link added:** True. Between European Union countries there are no customs inspections, so this sign is purely informative: it announces the border but imposes no stop. Do not confuse it with [signs posted at a checkpoint], which do require stopping.

## 21339 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica un posto telefonico pubblico nelle vicinanze*

The sign shown indicates a public telephone nearby.

**Link added:** False. Service signs are told apart by their pictogram: the pump means fuel, while a public telephone is marked by [the handset symbol].

## 21297 (FALSO)

*Il segnale A di figura indica che è obbligatorio svoltare a sinistra*

Sign A in the figure indicates that a left turn is mandatory.

**Link added:** False. Sign A is the *senso unico parallelo* sign with the arrow pointing left. It tells you that the street on the left is one-way in that direction. It informs and imposes no turn. An order to turn comes from a mandatory sign, which is [a white arrow in a blue circle].

## 21365 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preavvisa a destra un passaggio a livello senza barriere*

The sign shown announces the upcoming railroad crossing without barriers on the right.

**Link added:** False. This is an information sign for a road junction: the right branch simply leads to another road (here the station and town center). A railroad crossing is where the road crosses railroad tracks, and it is warned by a red-triangle danger sign ([a locomotive for one without barriers], or [the St. Andrew's cross]), never by a junction direction sign like this.

## 21367 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che non è possibile svoltare a sinistra al primo incrocio*

The sign shown indicates that it is not possible to turn left at the first intersection.

**Link added:** True. [A no-entry symbol] marks the left turn at the first junction.

## 21391 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta agli autocarri di massa a pieno carico superiore a 3,5 tonnellate di proseguire diritto per Avezzano*

The sign shown prohibits trucks with a gross weight exceeding 3.5 metric tons from going straight to Avezzano.

**Link added:** True. [The no-truck-over-3.5t symbol] is placed on the straight-ahead arrow toward Avezzano.

## 21394 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta agli autocarri di massa a pieno carico superiore a 3,5 tonnellate di continuare diritto per raggiungere il centro urbano*

The sign shown prohibits trucks with a gross weight exceeding 3.5 metric tons from continuing straight to reach the urban center.

**Link added:** True. On advance junction signs an inserted prohibition symbol binds only the branch and the vehicles it shows: here [the no-trucks-over-3.5-t disc] sits on the straight-ahead branch toward the center, so those trucks may not continue straight.

## 21398 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che la corsia centrale è chiusa*

The sign shown indicates that the center lane is closed.

**Link added:** False. The arrows on the board are the branches of the junction ahead and not lanes. [The round symbol on the straight-ahead branch] bars that branch to trucks over 3.5 t. Every other vehicle may go straight, and nothing is closed.

## 21401 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica la presenza di un passaggio a livello svoltando a destra*

The sign shown indicates the presence of a railroad crossing by turning right.

**Link added:** True. [The level-crossing warning triangle] is on the right-turn branch.

## 21404 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preavvisa di svoltare a destra per S. Giovanni*

The sign shown announces the upcoming right turn for S. Giovanni.

**Link added:** True. S. Giovanni appears on the branch that bends to the right, so that is the turn to take. Note that the same right branch also carries [the railroad crossing symbol], warning that a train line crosses that road.

## 21405 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un pericolo per chi svolta a destra per il possibile attraversamento di treni*

The sign shown announces a danger for those turning right due to possible train crossing.

**Link added:** True. [The level-crossing symbol] warns of possible trains for those turning right.

## 21409 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che non è possibile raggiungere S. Giovanni*

The sign shown indicates that it is not possible to reach S. Giovanni.

**Link added:** False. S. Giovanni is written on the right branch, so it is reached by turning right. [The triangle on that branch] warns that a railroad crossing lies down that road. It tells drivers to expect trains and does not close the road.

## 21432 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che non è più possibile cambiare corsia*

The sign shown indicates that it is no longer possible to change lanes.

**Link added:** False. Preselection signs stand where the lane lines are still broken, precisely so you can still move into the lane for your destination. The binding comes later, when the lines turn continuous and [the arrows on the pavement] take over.

## 21509 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato obbliga a montare le catene su tutte le quattro ruote*

The sign shown requires fitting chains on all four wheels.

**Link added:** False. [The round blue chained-tire disc] means the pass is open only to vehicles with snow chains (or winter tires). Chains go on the DRIVE wheels, at least the two wheels of the driven axle. They are never required on all four.

## 21514 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato raccomanda l'uso delle catene o di pneumatici invernali fino a Trafoi*

The sign shown recommends the use of chains or winter tires up to Trafoi.

**Link added:** True. On the CHIUSO board the chains symbol sits on a plain rectangle, which makes it a recommendation for the stretch still open, as far as Trafoi. Only on [the APERTO board], where it appears as a round blue disc, is it a condition for transit.

## 21518 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato vieta il transito ai veicoli che non hanno le catene da neve*

The sign shown bans vehicles that do not have snow chains.

**Link added:** False. Look at which panel carries the tire symbols. Here they sit on a plain white RECTANGLE on the 'closed' board: for the still-reachable stretch (as far as Trafoi) chains or winter tires are RECOMMENDED. Only the round BLUE DISC version, as on [the 'open' board], makes them a condition of transit.

## 21519 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che i veicoli per poter transitare debbono avere a bordo le catene*

The sign shown indicates that vehicles must carry chains on board in order to pass.

**Link added:** False. Carrying chains is not a standing duty: the road authority may order winter tires or chains on board for a given road (art. 6 of the Code), and where it has, [a blue circular sign] says so. This is an indication board. It reports that the pass is closed and that the road is usable as far as Trafoi, where chains or winter tires are recommended. Nothing on it makes chains a condition of passing. Such orders are common in winter, and a driver who meets that sign without chains on board or winter tires fitted may not go on, so in winter it is prudent to carry chains or fit winter tires.

## 21549 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che il prossimo svincolo autostradale si trova a 25 chilometri*

The sign shown indicates that the next autostrada exit is 25 kilometers away.

**Link added:** False. On a road-identification sign the number names the road itself: A25 is autostrada number 25, as [SS 2 is state road number 2]. The number is never a distance, so it says nothing about how far the next junction is. A distance to a junction is given by an exit advance sign, which prints the figure in meters or kilometers beside the exit name.

## 21561 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto in corrispondenza di un pronto soccorso*

The sign shown is placed at a emergency room.

**Link added:** True. The red cross alone is the first-aid post sign, placed at the facility with the arrow showing the way in. Do not confuse it with [the hospital sign, which adds a bed to the cross], or with the pharmacy, whose cross is green.

## 21582 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato fuori dei centri abitati si trova dopo il segnale di pericolo ATTRAVERSAMENTO PEDONALE*

The sign shown outside of town is found after the PEDESTRIAN CROSSING danger sign.

**Link added:** True. Outside town the crossing is announced twice: first [the warning triangle as advance notice], then this blue square posted at the stripes themselves.

## 21583 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato si trova direttamente in corrispondenza delle strisce dell'attraversamento pedonale*

The sign shown is located directly at the pedestrian crossing stripes.

**Link added:** True. The square blue version stands at the crossing itself, right at the zebra stripes; it is [the triangle version] that gives advance warning.

## 21630 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica un limite minimo di velocità*

The sign shown indicates a minimum speed limit.

**Link added:** False. The blue square without a red border is only the advised speed in good conditions: you may lawfully go slower. A minimum speed would be [a round blue disc with the number], which is a mandatory sign.

## 21670 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica una pista ciclabile*

The sign shown indicates a bike path.

**Link added:** False. This is the bike crossing sign: a blue square showing a person riding a bicycle, placed at the stripes where cyclists cross the carriageway, and there drivers yield to them. The trap is that [the bike path sign (pista ciclabile)] looks very similar: it is a blue circle showing a bicycle with no rider. The circle is an obligation sign that reserves the path that follows for bicycles, while the square marks only the point where cyclists cross. Look at the shape first: a square is a crossing, a circle is a path.

## 21703 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica come utilizzare le corsie di una carreggiata*

The sign shown indicates how to use the lanes of a carriageway.

**Link added:** True. Lane-use signs diagram the carriageway and place a symbol on each lane to say how it may be used: here [the no-trucks-over-3.5-t disc] bars the left lane to those trucks, while every other vehicle may use both lanes.

## 21713 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica le velocità minime obbligatorie da mantenere sulle corsie indicate*

The sign shown indicates mandatory minimum speeds to be maintained on the indicated lanes.

**Link added:** True. [A blue circle with a number] is a mandatory minimum speed. On this green autostrada sign each disc sits on a lane and gives the minimum for that lane. The minimums rise toward the left, and a lane with no disc has no minimum.

## 21802 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato si trova al confine di Stato con un Paese dell'Unione Europea*

The sign shown is located at the State border with a country of the European Union.

**Link added:** True. This version without a distance panel stands at the border itself; the ring of EU stars with the country name identifies the state border with an EU member. [The advance version] adds the distance instead.

## 21283 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica un senso unico frontale*

The sign shown indicates a one-way street running straight ahead (senso unico frontale).

**Link added:** True. The upward white arrow on a blue SQUARE is the SENSO UNICO FRONTALE: the road ahead is one-way, in your direction. Watch the shape: [the same white arrow on a blue CIRCLE] is the mandatory sign 'direzione obbligatoria diritto' (you must go straight). Square = information about the road; circle = an order to you.

## 21287 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica l'obbligo di proseguire diritto*

The sign shown indicates the obligation to proceed straight ahead.

**Link added:** False. Shape decides: an order to go straight is [the white arrow on a blue CIRCLE (direzione obbligatoria diritto)]. This is the white arrow on a blue SQUARE, an information sign: the road ahead is one-way in your direction (senso unico frontale). It tells you about the road, it does not order you straight.

## 21554 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato se barrato da una striscia rossa indica la fine del centro abitato*

The sign shown if crossed by a red stripe indicates the end of the town.

**Link added:** True. The white panel with the town's name marks the start of the town. [The same name crossed by a red diagonal stripe] marks its end: you are leaving the town, and the urban rules no longer apply.

## 21556 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto per indicare la fine di una provincia*

The sign shown is placed to indicate the end of a province.

**Link added:** False. A town name on a white panel marks the start of a town (*centro abitato*), and from there the urban rules apply. The end of the town is marked by [the same name crossed by a red diagonal stripe]. The sign has nothing to do with the boundary of a province.
