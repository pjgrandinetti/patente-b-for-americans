## 22550 (FALSO)

*La distanza fra autotreni, in presenza di segnali di divieto di sorpasso fra di loro, deve essere pari ad almeno 10 metri*

The distance between truck-and-trailer combinations (autotreni), where signs prohibit them from passing one another, must be at least 10 meters.

**Link added:** False. Heavy goods vehicles over 3.5 t subject to [a mutual no-passing rule] on a single-lane-per-direction extra-urban road must stay at least 100 m apart, not 10. It is the other fixed distance of this topic, alongside the 20 m for snowplows.

## 22562 (FALSO)

*La distanza di sicurezza è commisurata anche al limite di velocità riportato sul retro del veicolo che ci precede*

Safe following distance is commensurate also with the speed limit shown on the rear of the vehicle ahead.

**Link added:** False. The safe distance depends on how fast the two vehicles are actually going, on braking ability and on the road, and the vehicle ahead's real speed is what counts. [The limit painted on its rear] is the maximum that vehicle is allowed by law, a fact about the vehicle's class, and says nothing about its speed at this moment; a truck marked 80 may be doing 40. So that marking is not one of the things the distance is measured against.

## 22568 (FALSO)

*La distanza di sicurezza deve essere di almeno 100 metri in caso di nevicata in atto*

Safe distance must be at least 100 meters in case of active snowfall.

**Link added:** False. Snow cuts tire grip and lengthens braking, so the distance must increase, but by judgment, not by a fixed 100 m rule. The only fixed figures in this topic are 20 m from working snowplows or salt-spreaders and 100 m between heavy trucks under [a mutual no-passing rule].

## 22658 (FALSO)

*La distanza di sicurezza deve essere di almeno 100 metri, in caso di forte pioggia*

The safe following distance must be at least 100 meters in case of heavy rain.

**Link added:** False. Heavy rain requires a larger gap because water cuts grip and lengthens braking, but no fixed 100 m rule exists; the only fixed figures in this topic are 20 m from working snowplows or salt-spreaders and 100 m between heavy trucks under [a mutual no-passing rule].
