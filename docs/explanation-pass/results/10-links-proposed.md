## 23466 (VERO)

*Il conducente che intende sorpassare deve accertarsi che nessun segnale verticale vieti la manovra*

The driver intending to overtake must ensure that no vertical sign prohibits the maneuver.

**Link added:** True. Checking for [a no-passing sign] is one of the mandatory preliminary checks, alongside visibility, carriageway width, a clear road to finish, and the vehicles ahead and behind.

## 23469 (FALSO)

*Il conducente che intende sorpassare deve unicamente accertarsi che non vi sia nessun segnale di divieto di sorpasso*

The driver intending to overtake must solely ensure that there is no overtaking prohibition sign.

**Link added:** False. The trick is 'unicamente' (solely): overtaking requires multiple checks beyond just the absence of [a prohibition sign], including safe distance, visibility, and road conditions. Read the whole sentence: strike «unicamente» and what is left is true; the added words are what make the statement false.

## 23510 (VERO)

*E' vietato il sorpasso di un veicolo che si sia arrestato in corrispondenza di un passaggio pedonale per consentire l'attraversamento di pedoni*

Overtaking a vehicle that has stopped at a pedestrian crossing to allow pedestrians to cross is prohibited.

**Link added:** True. The stopped vehicle screens the pedestrians it is yielding to, so overtaking it would carry you into their path. This ban holds even away from [a marked crossing].

## 23513 (VERO)

*E' vietato il sorpasso se si deve valicare la striscia longitudinale bianca continua semplice o doppia*

Overtaking is prohibited if the single or double continuous white line must be crossed.

**Link added:** True. [A continuous line], single or [double], may never be crossed to pass; only [a dashed line] may. Where a dashed line runs alongside a solid one, the line nearer to you decides.

## 23520 (VERO)

*E' obbligatorio accertarsi prima di effettuare il sorpasso che non vi siano segnali che lo vietino*

It is mandatory to ensure before performing an overtake that there are no prohibiting signs.

**Link added:** True. Confirming that [no sign forbids the pass] is one of the mandatory checks before starting, together with visibility, carriageway width, a clear road to finish, and the vehicles ahead and behind.

## 23522 (VERO)

*E' vietato il sorpasso se il veicolo da superare si è fermato per dare precedenza ai pedoni, anche fuori dagli attraversamenti pedonali*

Overtaking a vehicle that has stopped to yield to pedestrians is prohibited, even outside pedestrian crossings.

**Link added:** True. The ban applies even outside [marked crossings]: the vehicle stopped to yield screens the pedestrians from your view, and passing it would cross their path.

## 23541 (FALSO)

*Il sorpasso è vietato se si è costretti a superare la linea longitudinale tratteggiata*

Overtaking is prohibited if forced to cross the dashed longitudinal line.

**Link added:** False. [The dashed line] is precisely the marking that permits crossing to overtake; only [a continuous line], single or [double], forbids it.

## 23553 (FALSO)

*È vietato il sorpasso in corrispondenza dei passi carrabili*

Overtaking is prohibited at driveway entrances.

**Link added:** False. Driveway entrances are not on the list of places where overtaking is forbidden; the real bans cover curves and crests on two-way roads, poor visibility, [continuous lines], acceleration and deceleration lanes, and queues where the oncoming lane would be needed.

## 23554 (FALSO)

*È vietato il sorpasso in prossimità degli ospedali*

Overtaking is prohibited near hospitals.

**Link added:** False. Nearness to a hospital does not forbid overtaking; the bans depend on visibility and road layout, such as curves and crests on two-way roads, [continuous lines], and passes that need the oncoming lane.

## 23555 (FALSO)

*È vietato il sorpasso in prossimità delle stazioni di servizio*

Overtaking is prohibited near service stations.

**Link added:** False. Service stations are not on the list of forbidden places; the bans hinge on visibility and road layout, not on what stands beside the road: curves and crests on two-way roads, [continuous lines], and passes that need the oncoming lane.

## 23579 (FALSO)

*La pericolosità del sorpasso è determinata dalla presenza, sulla strada, di delineatori normali di margine*

Among the dangers of overtaking is the presence of standard edge markers on the road.

**Link added:** False. [Ordinary edge markers] are fixed roadside delineators and create no danger. The dangers are moving conflicts: oncoming traffic, the overtaken vehicle when you return, vehicles behind that start to pass, and a narrow carriageway.

## 23597 (VERO)

*E' consentito sorpassare un'auto della Polizia, se non è in servizio di emergenza e non vi sono altri tipi di impedimenti*

It is permitted to overtake a police car, if it is not in emergency service and there are no other types of restrictions.

**Link added:** True. Police vehicles enjoy no special protection from being overtaken: the ordinary rules decide. Only an emergency response changes that, and any other ban, such as a curve or [a continuous line], still applies.
