## 18926 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato può essere integrato con pannello con la scritta TORNANTE*

The sign shown can be supplemented with a panel reading "TORNANTE" (hairpin turn).

**Link added:** True. Either curve sign may carry [a TORNANTE panel]: it tells you the bend ahead is a hairpin, tighter than an ordinary curve and demanding an even lower speed.

## 18978 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato può essere integrato con un pannello indicante TORNANTI*

The sign shown can be supplemented with a panel reading "TORNANTI" (hairpin turns).

**Link added:** True. It may carry [a TORNANTI panel]: the bends ahead are hairpins, tighter than ordinary curves and demanding an even lower speed.

## 18990 (FALSO)

*Il segnale con il pannello in figura preannuncia un tratto di strada lungo 3,8 km con una serie di curve pericolose*

The sign with the panel shown warns of a section of road 3.8 km long with a series of dangerous curves.

**Link added:** False. The panel carries a bare distance with no arrows, so it tells you the danger begins 3.8 km further on; it does not measure a 3.8 km stretch of road. The extent of a hazard is given by [the other panel, the one with an arrow at each end], and the triangle above announces a double curve, not a whole series of bends.

## 19005 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia la presenza di tornanti se integrato con apposito pannello*

The sign shown warns of hairpin turns when supplemented with the appropriate panel.

**Link added:** True. With [a TORNANTI panel] it warns of hairpin bends: tighter than ordinary curves, they demand an even lower speed.

## 19028 (VERO)

*Dopo il segnale raffigurato, se ci sono le semibarriere, è installato un dispositivo a luci rosse lampeggianti*

After the sign shown, if there are half-barriers, a device with flashing red lights is installed.

**Link added:** True. Half-barriers are paired with [a device of flashing red lights]: when they flash a train is coming and you must stop, never zigzagging between the bars.

## 19030 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un attraversamento ferroviario munito di barriere o semibarriere*

The sign shown warns of a railroad crossing equipped with barriers or half-barriers.

**Link added:** True. The fence symbol is the distinguishing mark: it announces a crossing protected by barriers or half-barriers, whatever the number of tracks. The crossing without barriers is announced by [a different sign, showing a locomotive].

## 19034 (VERO)

*Dopo il segnale raffigurato, se ci sono le barriere, è installato un dispositivo a luce rossa fissa*

After the sign shown, if there are barriers, a device with steady red light is installed.

**Link added:** True. Full barriers are paired with a steady red light, while half-barriers get [flashing red lights]: in both cases red means stop until the crossing reopens.

## 19042 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato si trova prima del segnale verticale DOPPIA CROCE DI S. ANDREA*

The sign shown is found before the DOUBLE ST. ANDREW'S CROSS vertical sign.

**Link added:** False. [The St Andrew's cross] is used at crossings without barriers; this is a barriered crossing, so no such cross follows.

## 19047 (VERO)

*In presenza del segnale raffigurato è necessario arrestarsi se sono in funzione le due luci rosse lampeggianti*

Where this sign is posted, you must stop if the two flashing red lights are operating.

**Link added:** True. [The flashing red lights] mean the half-barriers are closing or closed because a train is coming: you must stop even if the bars have not yet come down, and you may never zigzag between them.

## 19055 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto, di norma, a 150 metri dai binari, integrato dal relativo pannello distanziometrico*

The sign shown is normally placed 150 meters from the tracks, supplemented with the corresponding distance panel.

**Link added:** True. It normally stands about 150 m from the tracks and rides on [the first countdown panel, the one with three red bars]; the panels then count down as you approach.

## 19058 (VERO)

*Dopo il segnale raffigurato è installato il segnale CROCE DI S. ANDREA, che è posizionato prima dei binari*

After the sign shown the ST. ANDREW'S CROSS sign is installed, which is positioned before the tracks.

**Link added:** True. Nearer the tracks [the St Andrew's cross sign] is installed, just before the rails.

## 19059 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è integrato con il pannello distanziometrico a tre barre rosse*

The sign shown is supplemented with the distance panel with three red bars.

**Link added:** True. [The three-bar panel] is the farthest of the countdown panels, so it carries the warning triangle; the bars drop to two and then one as you near the tracks.

## 19060 (VERO)

*Dopo il segnale raffigurato è installato il segnale DOPPIA CROCE DI S. ANDREA, se la linea ferroviaria ha più di un binario*

After the sign shown the DOUBLE ST. ANDREW'S CROSS sign is installed, if the rail line has more than one track.

**Link added:** True. [The single St. Andrew's cross] marks a line with one track; [the double cross] tells you there is more than one track, so after one train has passed another may arrive from either direction. The cross stands right before the tracks, after this advance sign.

## 19078 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che la sede ferroviaria ha un solo binario*

The sign shown indicates that the rail line has a single track.

**Link added:** True. A single cross means the line has one track; [the doubled cross] is used for two or more tracks, where another train may follow from either direction.

## 19079 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto nelle immediate vicinanze di un attraversamento ferroviario senza barriere*

The sign shown is placed in the immediate vicinity of a railroad crossing without barriers.

**Link added:** True. Unlike [the advance warning sign placed about 150 meters ahead], the St. Andrew's cross stands right at the track. Its single cross also tells you the line has just one track.

## 19081 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto sulla strada dopo il segnale PASSAGGIO A LIVELLO SENZA BARRIERE*

The sign shown is placed on the road after the RAILROAD CROSSING WITHOUT BARRIERS sign.

**Link added:** True. The approach sequence is fixed: first [the advance triangle], then [the countdown panels], and finally the cross immediately before the rails.

## 19082 (VERO)

*La figura rappresenta un segnale di pericolo*

The figure represents a warning sign.

**Link added:** True. The St. Andrew's cross belongs to the warning family even though it is not a triangle: this family uses three shapes, the triangle, [the countdown panels], and this cross at the crossing itself.

## 19084 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una sede ferroviaria con più binari*

The sign shown warns of a rail line with multiple tracks.

**Link added:** False. A single cross means one track; multiple tracks use [the double cross].

## 19086 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una sede tranviaria con un solo binario*

The sign shown warns of a tram line with a single track.

**Link added:** False. The St. Andrew's cross stands at a railroad crossing; a tram line crossing the road is announced by [its own triangular warning sign]. The single cross does mean one track, but of a rail line.

## 19089 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è integrato da una luce verde ed una rossa fissa*

The sign shown is supplemented by a green light and a steady red light.

**Link added:** False. The St. Andrew's cross is a red-and-white X with no lights of its own. The light devices at railroad crossings are red only: one steady red light, or [two red lights that flash alternately]. A pair made of a green light and a steady red one is not among them.

## 19091 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato impone di arrestarsi entro la striscia di arresto, se è in arrivo il treno.*

The sign shown requires you to stop before the stop line if a train is approaching.

**Link added:** True. The cross is the one warning sign that also imposes yielding: if a train may be approaching you must stop before [the stop line] and let it pass.

## 19092 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto nelle immediate vicinanze di un attraversamento ferroviario a livello senza barriere*

The sign shown is placed in the immediate vicinity of a railroad crossing without barriers.

**Link added:** True. The double St. Andrew's cross stands immediately before the tracks, not 150 meters ahead like [the advance warning sign]. Its doubled arms tell you the line has more than one track, so watch for a second train after the first has passed.

## 19093 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica che la linea ferroviaria ha più di un binario*

The sign shown indicates that the rail line has more than one track.

**Link added:** True. The doubled arms mean two or more tracks, against [the single cross] for one track: after one train has passed, another may arrive, even from the other direction.

## 19095 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato si trova dopo i pannelli distanziometrici a barre rosse*

The sign shown is found after the distance-indicating panels with red bars.

**Link added:** True. [The countdown panels] line the approach, their bars decreasing from three to one, and the cross comes last, immediately before the rails.

## 19096 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è un segnale di pericolo*

The sign shown is a warning sign.

**Link added:** True. It is a warning sign even though it is not a triangle: the family uses three shapes, the triangle, [the countdown panels], and this cross standing at the crossing itself.

## 19099 (FALSO)

*Il segnale (A) si trova dopo il segnale (B)*

Sign (A) is found after sign (B).

**Link added:** False. The doubled St. Andrew's cross stands at the crossing itself, but only at crossings without barriers; sign (B), with the gate symbol, announces a crossing with barriers. Where barriers protect the tracks no cross is placed, so sign (A) follows [the locomotive triangle], never sign (B).

## 19112 (FALSO)

*I pannelli raffigurati sono segnali di prescrizione*

The panels shown are regulatory signs.

**Link added:** False. They belong to the warning family, one of its three shapes together with the triangle and [the St. Andrew's cross]: they only signal the approach and prescribe nothing.

## 19148 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato può essere posto anche nei centri abitati*

The sign shown may also be placed in town.

**Link added:** True. Even in town the advance triangle is normal; do not confuse it with [the blue square sign], which marks the crossing itself rather than warning of it.

## 19187 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto prima di una discesa pericolosa*

The sign shown is placed before a dangerous descent.

**Link added:** True. The symbol shows the slope falling in your direction of travel: it is a descent, not the climb announced by [the similar uphill-grade sign]. It stands before the drop so you can slow down and engage a low gear in time.

## 19190 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una discesa e ne specifica la pendenza*

The sign shown warns of a descent and specifies its grade.

**Link added:** True. The number on the sign is the steepness of the descent, expressed as a grade percentage. It is not a speed limit, and it does not refer to a climb: [the uphill grade has its own separate sign].

## 19193 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una salita da percorrere con marcia adeguatamente bassa*

The sign shown warns of an uphill stretch to be traveled in an appropriately low gear.

**Link added:** False. The slope in the symbol falls in the direction of travel: it is the descent sign. The steep climb has [its own mirror-image sign] with the same percentage.

## 19209 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato è posto prima di una salita ripida*

The sign shown is placed before a steep uphill stretch.

**Link added:** True. The symbol's slope rises in the direction of travel: it announces a steep climb, the mirror image of [the dangerous-descent sign] with the same percentage.

## 19212 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato specifica la pendenza della salita*

The sign shown specifies the grade of the uphill stretch.

**Link added:** True. The percentage on the sign is the steepness of the climb, its grade: it is not a speed limit and does not refer to [a descent, which has its own sign].

## 19216 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una discesa e ne specifica la pendenza*

The sign shown warns of a descent and specifies its grade.

**Link added:** False. The slope in the symbol rises in the direction of travel, so it announces a climb; [the descent sign] is its mirror image with the same percentage.

## 19250 (VERO)

## 19375 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia il pericolo di trovare pietrisco sulla pavimentazione stradale*

The sign shown warns of the danger of finding loose gravel on the road surface.

**Link added:** True. Loose gravel on the surface cuts tire grip and is flung by passing wheels, so moderate speed when meeting other vehicles and expect skidding. Do not confuse it with [falling rocks]: that sign concerns stones falling from above, here they already lie on the road.

## 19386 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un tratto di strada ove esiste il pericolo di presenza di pietre sulla carreggiata*

The sign shown warns of a stretch where stones may be lying on the carriageway.

**Link added:** True. The hazard is not only being struck: fallen stones sit on the road as obstacles, so keep your speed low enough to spot and avoid them. The pavement itself is sound; this is not [the sign for a broken-up or rough road].

## 19387 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia il pericolo di caduta di pietre da sinistra con conseguente loro presenza sulla carreggiata*

The sign shown warns of stones falling from the left and ending up on the carriageway.

**Link added:** True. The symbol shows which side the rocks come from, here the left; [a mirror version] exists for falls from the right. Fallen stones may already lie on the carriageway.

## 19395 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia il pericolo di caduta di massi da destra*

The sign shown warns of danger of falling rocks from the right.

**Link added:** True. The side of the symbol with the rocky cliff shows where the rocks come from: here the rock face is on the right, and [a mirror version] exists for falls from the left. Slow down and expect stones already lying on the carriageway.

## 19411 (VERO)

The sign shown warns of a traffic light with vertically arranged lights.

**Link added:** True. The symbol reproduces the layout of the signal you will actually meet: red, yellow and green discs stacked vertically. [A separate version of the sign] exists for lights arranged horizontally, so the sign tells you what installation to expect.

## 19436 (FALSO)

The sign shown warns of the presence of low-flying aircraft.

## 19455 (FALSO)

The sign shown warns of possible ice on the road.

**Link added:** False. The windsock announces wind, not ice: possible ice is announced by [the slippery-road sign with its supplementary panel]. The hazard here is being pushed sideways by sudden gusts.

## 19505 (FALSO)

## 18854 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una strada in cattivo stato*

The sign shown warns of a road in poor condition.

**Link added:** True. This warning triangle, normally placed 150 meters ahead, announces a stretch whose surface is uneven or broken up, so you slow down to spare the suspension and keep control. It may be paired with [a speed limit], and on a yellow background it marks road work sites.

## 18857 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un tratto di strada dissestata*

The sign shown warns of a broken-up stretch of road.

**Link added:** True. The sign covers a generally broken-up, irregular surface, not a single change of grade: [a bump (dosso)] and [a dip (cunetta)] each have their own distinct warning sign.

## 18852 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica un tratto di strada deformata lungo 380 metri*

The sign shown indicates a stretch of rough road 380 meters long.

**Link added:** True. Tell the two panel types apart by the arrows: a panel with arrows at both ends gives the LENGTH of the stretch (here 380 m); [a plain figure] gives the DISTANCE to where the hazard begins.

## 18868 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica un tratto di strada deformata lungo 320 metri*

The sign shown indicates a stretch of rough road for 320 meters.

**Link added:** False. The panel under the triangle is a bare figure with no arrows, and that form states the DISTANCE from the sign to where the hazard begins: the deformed stretch starts 320 meters ahead. The statement instead claims the rough road is 320 meters long, which the panel never says. [A panel that gives the LENGTH of the affected stretch] carries an arrow at each end of the figure, and this one does not.

## 18884 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia una variazione di pendenza della strada con limitata visibilità*

The sign shown warns of a change in road grade with limited visibility.

**Link added:** True. A bump is an uphill stretch followed by a downhill one, and the crest hides the road beyond: that lack of sight distance is the real danger the sign announces. It says nothing about the quality of the surface, and [a broken-up road] has its own sign.

## 18887 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un tratto di strada in cattivo stato*

The sign shown warns of a road section in poor condition.

**Link added:** False. This triangle warns of a bump, a rise followed by a descent that limits visibility. [A road in poor condition, with a broken or irregular surface], is announced by a different danger sign; the two are deliberately easy to mix up.

## 18906 (VERO)

*Il segnale raffigurato si trova prima di un tratto in discesa seguito da uno in salita*

The sign shown is found before a downhill stretch followed by an uphill stretch.

**Link added:** True. A dip is a descent followed by a rise, the exact opposite profile of [the bump]: the two signs mirror each other and are a classic look-alike pair.

## 18910 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato preannuncia un tratto di strada in cattivo stato*

The sign shown warns of a road stretch in poor condition.

**Link added:** False. The sign warns of a dip, a short descent followed by a rise, a spot that can even flood in heavy rain. It does not speak about the state of the surface: [a broken-up road] has its own distinct warning sign.

## 18912 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato può trovarsi prima di un tratto in salita seguito da una discesa*

The sign shown may be found before an uphill stretch followed by a downhill stretch.

**Link added:** False. A dip is a descent then a rise; a rise then a descent is [a bump].
