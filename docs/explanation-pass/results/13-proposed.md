To turn left one must turn around the center of the intersection, like the vehicles in the figure.

**Link added:** False. Going around the intersection's center belongs to signed roundabouts and central islands. The normal left turn does the opposite: [you keep the center point on your right and turn in front of it], near the median.

## 22860 (FALSO)

*Su una strada a doppio senso di marcia per svoltare a sinistra è necessario spostarsi a sinistra della linea continua di mezzeria*

On a two-way road to turn left it is necessary to move to the left of the continuous center line.

**Link added:** False. To turn left on a two-way road you move up close to the center line and stay on your own side of it, on its right; you never move to its left before turning. And a [CONTINUOUS center line] may never be crossed or straddled at all, so where it stays continuous you may not turn left there. Where a left turn is allowed, at a junction or at a driveway, the center line is [broken] or interrupted.

## 22929 (VERO)

*Il conducente che intende effettuare l'inversione di marcia su una strada a doppio senso deve rinunciare ad eseguire la manovra, se vi è scarsa visibilità*

The driver who intends to perform a U-turn on a two-way road must refrain from performing the maneuver if there is poor visibility.

**Link added:** True. In poor visibility an oncoming vehicle can appear when you are already broadside across its lane. No marking overrides this: even with a [dashed center line], poor visibility rules the U-turn out.

## 22935 (FALSO)

*Il conducente che intende effettuare l'inversione di marcia su una strada a doppio senso può compiere la manovra anche con scarsa visibilità, se la linea di mezzeria è tratteggiata*

The driver who intends to perform a U-turn on a two-way road may perform the maneuver even with poor visibility if the center line is dashed.

**Link added:** False. A U-turn may not be performed in poor visibility, even with a [dashed center line]; visibility conditions override the line marking.

## 22939 (VERO)

*Nelle strade a doppio senso non è consentito effettuare l'inversione del senso di marcia in tutti i casi di scarsa visibilità*

On two-way roads it is not permitted to perform a U-turn in all cases of poor visibility.

**Link added:** True. Poor visibility, whatever its cause, bans the U-turn outright: a vehicle can appear when you are already across its lane. Not even a [dashed center line] overrides this.

## 22942 (FALSO)

*Qualora la linea di margine della carreggiata sia continua, non è consentito effettuare l'inversione di marcia*

If the edge line of the carriageway is continuous, it is not permitted to perform a U-turn.

**Link added:** False. The trap is the line it names. The edge line (linea di margine) marks the right-hand side of the carriageway, and a U-turn never crosses it, so it cannot forbid one. The continuous line that does forbid a U-turn is the [CENTER line (linea di mezzeria)]: it may never be crossed, not even to turn around. Apart from that, a U-turn is forbidden near curves, crests and intersections, wherever visibility is poor, on one-way roads and always on the autostrada.

## 22947 (VERO)

*Negli incroci regolati con rotatoria, la circolazione può essere disciplinata in modo che i veicoli in entrata diano la precedenza a quelli già circolanti nell'anello*

In intersections regulated with a roundabout, traffic may be regulated so that entering vehicles yield to those already circulating in the ring.

**Link added:** True. This is what the [YIELD sign] posted at the entries does: it obliges entering vehicles to give way to those already circulating, reversing the unsigned default. Nearly every real roundabout is regulated this way.

## 22948 (VERO)

*In un incrocio regolato con circolazione rotatoria, l'obbligo di dare la precedenza può essere imposto ai veicoli che stanno per immettersi*

In an intersection regulated with roundabout circulation, the obligation to yield may be imposed on vehicles about to enter.

**Link added:** True. Priority at a roundabout is set by the signs: posting [YIELD] or [STOP] at the entries imposes the duty on vehicles about to enter, giving the ring priority. Without such signs the ordinary right-hand rule would apply instead.

## 22952 (FALSO)

*Giungendo ad un incrocio in cui la circolazione è regolata con rotatoria, è obbligatorio dare la precedenza ai veicoli provenienti da sinistra*

Reaching an intersection where traffic is regulated with a roundabout, it is mandatory to yield to vehicles coming from the left.

**Link added:** False. At a roundabout, you must yield to vehicles coming from the left only if there is a [YIELD sign]; without a specific sign, the general rule of yielding to the right applies. The statement incorrectly claims this is always mandatory.
