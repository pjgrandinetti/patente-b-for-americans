## 21910 (FALSO)

*Il segnale raffigurato indica la chiusura della corsia per i veicoli lenti*

The sign shown indicates the closure of the lane for slow vehicles.

**Link added:** False. The diagram closes the right lane for every vehicle, with no category symbol on it; assigning lanes by vehicle class is the job of [a different work-zone panel].

## 21946 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato è posto al centro della carreggiata per indicare un ostacolo*

The delineator shown is placed in the center of the carriageway to indicate an obstacle.

**Link added:** False. It lines the sides, never the center: obstacles on the carriageway are marked by [the obstacle delineator with the blue pass-this-side disc], a different device.

## 21948 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato è un pannello distanziometrico posto vicino ad un passaggio a livello*

The delineator shown is a countdown (distance) panel placed near a railroad crossing.

**Link added:** False. [The countdown panels near a railroad crossing] carry diagonal bars that count down the distance to the crossing. This is instead a side delineator for one-way tunnels, detours, and narrowings, with no connection to railroads.

## 21979 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato indica una curva a senso unico*

The delineator shown indicates a one-way curve.

**Link added:** False. [A chevron series pointing one way] marks a bend; this double version pointing both ways marks a T-junction where you choose left or right.

## 21980 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato indica l'ingresso di una galleria*

The delineator shown indicates the entrance to a tunnel.

**Link added:** False. Tunnel walls are lined by [the tall yellow tunnel delineator]; the double chevron barrier stands where a road ends at a T-junction and turns you left or right.

## 21961 (VERO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato si trova, di norma, dopo il segnale CURVA PERICOLOSA A SINISTRA*

The delineator shown is normally found after the DANGEROUS LEFT CURVE sign.

**Link added:** True. The two signs divide the work: [the DANGEROUS LEFT CURVE triangle] warns you in advance, then the chevrons in the bend itself show the way the road turns.

## 21967 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato indica gli ostacoli sporgenti sulla carreggiata*

The delineator shown indicates protruding obstacles on the carriageway.

**Link added:** False. Obstacles on the carriageway are marked by [the obstacle delineator with the blue pass-this-side disc]; the chevron panel instead traces the course of a sharp bend.

## 21986 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato si trova 150 metri prima di una curva*

The delineator shown is located 150 meters before a curve.

**Link added:** False. It is not an advance sign: [the DANGEROUS BEND triangle] warns before the curve, while the chevron stands in the bend itself, tracing it as you drive.

## 21987 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato è posto all'ingresso di una galleria*

The delineator shown is placed at the entrance to a tunnel.

**Link added:** False. Tunnel walls are lined by [the tall yellow tunnel delineator]; the chevron element instead sits in a sharp bend, used in series to trace its course.

## 21988 (FALSO)

*Il delineatore raffigurato viene usato nei passaggi a livello, quando le barriere sono guaste*

The delineator shown is used at railroad crossings when barriers are broken.

**Link added:** False. When crossing gates are out of order it is [the red-and-white barrier] that stands in for them; the chevron element marks a sharp bend, pointing the way it turns.

## 21864 (FALSO)

*La barriera raffigurata segnala una curva pericolosa*

The barrier shown signals a dangerous curve.

**Link added:** False. Dangerous bends are announced by warning signs and traced by [chevron markers]; the barrier instead closes off the area a work site occupies.

## 21871 (FALSO)

*Il cono raffigurato si può usare in presenza di lavori stradali superiori a 10 giorni*

The cone shown can be used in the presence of road work lasting more than 10 days.

**Link added:** False. Cones are only for short-duration or moving situations; a site lasting more than 10 days needs sturdier fixed delimitation, such as [barriers].

## 21872 (FALSO)

*Il cono raffigurato si usa su strade dove avvengono abbondanti nevicate*

The cone shown is used on roads where heavy snowfall occurs.

**Link added:** False. Snowy mountain roads get their own device, [the black-and-yellow snow delineator] that keeps the edges visible; cones mark short jobs, detours, and accident scenes.

## 21927 (FALSO)

*I delineatori raffigurati sono posti al centro della carreggiata*

The delineators shown are placed at the center of the carriageway.

**Link added:** False. Edge delineators always stand at the margins and never in the center; the only delineator placed on the carriageway is [the obstacle delineator], a different device.
