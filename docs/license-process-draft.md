English only, for review. The Italian follows once the English is settled.
Nothing in the app has changed yet.

## Opening (shared, above the tabs)

Getting a Category B license in Italy takes several stages, and this app
prepares you for one of them, the theory exam. You can go through a driving
school (autoscuola), which files your paperwork and books your exams, or apply
as a private candidate (privatista) and do that yourself. Most immigrants pick
a school and study the theory here instead of in the school's Italian class,
since attending a theory course is not required.

Either way, you must be at least 18 and resident in Italy. A non-EU citizen
must also show a valid residence permit (permesso di soggiorno).

## Tab 1. Through a driving school

1. **Enroll.** Choose an authorized driving school and sign up. The school
   tells you which documents and medical papers to bring. There is no single
   directory of schools, because they are licensed locally, so search your
   area and confirm the school is authorized.

2. **Get the medical-history note.** Your own doctor (medico di base) writes
   the certificato anamnestico. Your doctor charges for this note, because the
   national health service does not cover it. Expect to pay about €50.

3. **Pass the medical.** You book the visit with a Ministry-authorized doctor
   through the school, usually on its own premises. Bring the certificato
   anamnestico, since the doctor cannot issue the certificate without it. The
   doctor checks your fitness and eyesight and issues the medical certificate.

4. **The school files your application.** It submits the TT2112 form to your
   provincial Motorizzazione office with your documents, the medical
   certificate and the fee receipts. The medical certificate must be under
   3 months old when the application is filed. From then on you have 6 months
   and 2 attempts to pass the theory exam.

5. **Pass the theory exam.** It has 30 true/false questions in 20 minutes,
   and you may get no more than 3 wrong. It is offered in Italian, with French
   and German versions, and never in English. It is held in computerized rooms
   at the Motorizzazione, not at the school. This is what this app prepares
   you for.

   The school decides when to put you forward, and most will not do it until
   they have seen a good average on their own practice sheets, taken in the
   school's own app. Most schools use a publisher's textbook and practice app,
   and the school reads your averages there. Your work here is not visible to them, so plan on running
   their sheets as well once your study is done. This is the school's own
   condition and not a Ministry rule. Ask early what average it wants and over
   how many sheets, and how far ahead the exam dates are running.

6. **Get your foglio rosa and take the mandatory lessons.** Passing the
   theory exam earns the foglio rosa, a learner permit valid 12 months.
   Before you may practice privately, you must complete 8 hours of certified
   lessons at the school, in four modules. They are 2 hours of vehicle
   handling and emergency drills, 3 hours of city driving, 2 hours on the
   autostrada or extra-urban roads, and 1 hour at night. After the school
   certifies them, you may practice with a companion who sits beside you, is
   no older than 65, and has held a B license for at least 10 years. The car
   must display a "P" plate.

7. **Pass the driving test.** The school books it and provides the car, which
   must have dual controls. You may take it from 1 month after the foglio
   rosa, with up to 3 attempts within its 12 months. A Motorizzazione examiner
   checks that you prepare the car for safe driving, watches two maneuvers,
   one of them in reverse, and rides with you for at least 25 minutes in
   normal traffic. Taking the test on an automatic is allowed, but the license
   then carries code 78 and is valid only for automatics.

8. **Receive your license.** (Text as today: what B covers, validity
   periods, the three-year neopatentato limits.)

**Cost.** There is no single fee. The certificato anamnestico costs about
€50. The school's enrollment and paperwork fee usually runs €150–300, more in
big cities, and it normally includes the Ministry fees. Lessons are usually
sold in 30-minute units of about €30 each, less in packages, so the 8
mandatory hours come to roughly €400–500. All in, getting the license through
a school typically costs €800–1,500. Expect a small deposit when exams are
booked, forfeited for an unjustified absence, and a 24-hour cancellation rule
on lessons.

## Tab 2. As a private candidate

1. **Get the medical-history note.** Your own doctor (medico di base) writes
   the certificato anamnestico. Your doctor charges for this note, because the
   national health service does not cover it. Expect to pay about €50.

2. **Pass the medical.** You book a Ministry-authorized doctor yourself. That
   is the ASL medical office, booked through the CUP, or an authorized doctor
   who sees candidates at a driving school or a vehicle-paperwork agency
   (agenzia di pratiche auto). Bring the certificato anamnestico, which on its
   own does not count as the medical certificate. The doctor checks your
   fitness and eyesight and issues the certificate, which must be under
   3 months old when you file.

3. **Pay the fees.** Pay on pagoPA, through the Portale dell'Automobilista,
   in your own name. Enrolling is tariff N067, €26.40 plus €16 stamp duty,
   €42.40 in all. Keep the receipt with its IUV code. The driving test later
   needs tariff N019, €16.

4. **File your application.** Complete and sign the TT2112 form. Submit it to
   your provincial Motorizzazione office with these documents.
   - A copy of a valid ID, front and back.
   - Your codice fiscale or health card, front and back.
   - The medical certificate.
   - The pagoPA receipt.
   - A 33 × 40 mm photo.
   - A non-EU citizen also adds a copy of the residence permit.

   How you submit depends on the office. Some take it at the counter, and
   others by email as a single PDF, so check yours first. The office then
   tells you the date of your theory exam. From filing you have 6 months and
   2 attempts to pass it.

5. **Pass the theory exam.** It has 30 true/false questions in 20 minutes,
   and you may get no more than 3 wrong. It is offered in Italian, with French
   and German versions, and never in English. It is held in computerized rooms
   at the Motorizzazione. This is what this app prepares you for.

6. **Get your foglio rosa and take the mandatory lessons.** Passing the
   theory exam earns the foglio rosa, a learner permit valid 12 months. Even
   as a private candidate you must complete 8 hours of certified lessons at
   an authorized driving school, in the four modules listed for the school
   route. After the school certifies them, you may practice with a companion
   who sits beside you, is no older than 65, and has held a B license for at
   least 10 years. The car must display a "P" plate.

7. **Pass the driving test.** The test car must have dual controls, so in
   practice you take it in a driving school's car, and some offices require a
   private candidate to go through a school for this step. You may take it
   from 1 month after the foglio rosa, with up to 3 attempts within its
   12 months. Bring the same TT2112 file, now with your foglio rosa, your
   lesson record and the N019 receipt. The test itself is the same as on the
   school route.

8. **Receive your license.** (Text as today.)

**Cost.** This is the cheapest route and the most work. The certificato
anamnestico costs about €50, and the authorized doctor charges a fee of their
own. The Ministry fees are €42.40 to enroll and €16 for the driving test. The
8 mandatory lessons come to roughly €400–500, and the school charges for the
use of its car at the test.

   **Research of 2026-10-14 (sourced; UNCONFIRMED items marked):**
   - Authorized doctor: ASL roughly €20 to €70 (ASL Umbria 1, €20.40 for an
     A/B renewal; another ASL page reportedly €69.54, UNCONFIRMED); private
     doctors in a large city €79 to €98. Sources: uslumbria1.it (8-Rinnovo
     patenti normali), sicurauto.it, facile.it, cup24.it.
   - Theory exam date: assigned by the office. Pistoia takes the file by
     e-mail and replies with a date; Massa Carrara opens each month's
     calendar on the 15th of the month before. Procedures and waits differ
     by office, and large offices have had long backlogs.
   - Mandatory lessons: DM 17 Nov 2025 n. 294 sets 8 hours in four modules
     (A 2 h vehicle and emergencies first; B 3 h urban; C 2 h autostrada or
     extra-urban; D 1 h night), at most 2 hours a day, on a school's
     dual-control car. It applies only once an implementing decreto
     dirigenziale is in force, to fogli rosa issued after it: whether it
     applies today is UNCONFIRMED. The old rule (DM 20 Apr 2012) is 6 hours.
     The How-to states 8 hours in four modules as current; CHECK.
   - Driving test: dual controls and an instructor beside the candidate
     (MIT circular 28819 of 19/09/2019, cited by Massa Carrara). Pistoia
     requires a privatista to go through a school for the test. A rented
     dual-control car is reported allowed. Car and instructor for the test:
     €100 to €200 (commercial sources).
   - Private practice: art. 122 c. 2, companion up to 65 with a license of
     the category for 10 years, "P" sign; c. 5-bis (L. 177/2024) allows it
     only after the mandatory hours, possibly pending the CED platform.
   - Fees: N067 €26.40 + €16, N019 €16 (official office sheets). No fee on
     issue confirmed.
   - Totals reported (commercial): €300 to €760 for the private route,
     against €700 to €1,200 with a school; based on 6 hours of lessons.
7. **Add to the school route with the cost update** (maintainer,
   2026-10-14): a paragraph on choosing a school as an experienced driver.
   Packages are built for first-time drivers. Savings are in a package
   without the theory course (some schools sell paperwork and exam
   presentation only), in buying only the mandatory driving hours, and in
   lessons sold singly. Ask each school for a written price list, item by
   item: enrollment and paperwork; the theory course and whether it is
   optional; presenting you at the theory exam and the booking fee; cost
   per driving lesson and whether only the mandatory hours can be bought;
   presenting you at the driving test, car included; a second attempt at
   either exam. English-speaking schools exist mainly in large cities; name
   none without a verified source.
