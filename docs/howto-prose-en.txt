Getting a Category B license in Italy takes several stages, and this app prepares you for one of them, the theory exam. You can go through a driving school (autoscuola), which files your paperwork and books your exams, or apply as a private candidate (privatista) and do that yourself. Most immigrants enroll in a school for the paperwork and study the theory in this app, since the school's theory course is not required.

Either way, you must be at least 18 and resident in Italy. A non-EU citizen must also show a valid residence permit (permesso di soggiorno).

- Study the theory first. Work through the official questions in this app, in English and then in Italian. This usually takes more than 3 months. Some of the later steps have deadlines once they begin (step 3 explains them), so study the theory first. Once you are making good progress, you can start on the steps below.

- Decide between a driving school and the private route. The two routes differ a great deal in cost. The Cost paragraph of each route, below, gives the figures. Before you decide, visit two or three driving schools. They are licensed locally and there is no single directory, so search your area.

If you are considering enrolling in a school, ask:

- What does enrollment include? Ask for the price item by item. The items are the paperwork and the Motorizzazione fees, the visit to the Ministry-authorized doctor, the theory course, the school's mock exam app, the mandatory driving lessons, and the car for the driving test.

- May you study the theory on your own instead of attending the course? Schools usually follow your progress in their own app and decide from it when to put you forward for the theory exam. Ask whether your results in this app will be accepted as proof that you are ready. The Statistics page of this app shows your mock exams and your chance of failing. If not, ask what score on the school's own mock exams it wants first.

- Which publisher's textbook and software does the school use, and can it get you the textbook in English? Many schools work with SIDA, whose manual exists in an English edition.

- Does an instructor speak English well enough to answer your questions? A school with no language support can be an expensive choice of little value. Even with it, keep in mind that a driving school teaches the rules, not the language. Its theory course is not meant to help you with Italian grammar and vocabulary, so it may be of little use if the Italian is what you struggle with.

If you are considering the private route, you will still need a driving school once you have the foglio rosa. The mandatory driving lessons, 6 hours behind the wheel with an instructor, must be taken at a school. The official driving test is almost always taken in a school's dual-control car. Ask:

- Does the school give the mandatory driving lessons to a private candidate? What does a driving lesson cost, and how many hours is one lesson? What is the price of the 6 mandatory hours? If you have driven for years, the 6 hours may be all you need. If you have driven only automatics and want a license without code 78, plan on more, so the price of one lesson matters.

- How soon can the driving lessons be scheduled once you have the foglio rosa?

- Can the driving lessons and the driving test be taken in an automatic, if that is what you drive? The license then carries code 78.

- Does the school provide its car for a private candidate's official driving test, and what does it charge for using it?

- Get the certificato anamnestico (medical-history note). This is a certificate on your medical history. Your own doctor (medico di base) writes it from the records they hold, usually without examining you. The certificato anamnestico has one line for each of the conditions listed below, and your medico di base marks each line yes or no. The national health service does not cover the certificato anamnestico, so your medico di base will charge for it. Expect to pay about €50. You do not need to be enrolled at a driving school to get it.

When to get it. The certificato anamnestico is valid for 3 months, and it must still be valid when you file your application for the theory exam. The application then gives you 6 months to take the exam, with at most two attempts. So get the certificato anamnestico when you expect to pass the theory within about 6 months. For most learners, that is when they have finished the questions in English and are working through them in Italian. Then take the next steps of your route in the following weeks, and bring the certificato anamnestico with you.

What the certificato anamnestico asks.

- Heart and circulation (apparato cardio-circolatorio)

- Diabetes (diabete mellito)

- Endocrine system (sistema endocrino)

- Nervous system (apparato neurologico)

- Psychiatric conditions (patologie psichiche)

- Epilepsy (epilessia)

- Dependence on alcohol, drugs or psychoactive substances (condizioni di dipendenza da alcol/sostanze stupefacenti e psicotrope)

- Urinary and genital system, severe kidney failure (apparato urogenitale, insufficienza renale grave)

- Blood and blood-forming organs, meaning hemophilia, or a lymphoma or leukemia under treatment (sangue ed organi emopoietici)

- Bones and joints, serious anatomical or functional impairment (apparato osteoarticolare)

- Sense organs, serious eye disease that is getting worse (organi di senso)

Avoid a yes if you can. A yes on any line can delay your license by months. At the second medical visit, described in the routes below, a Ministry-authorized doctor (medico autorizzato) reads the certificato anamnestico. If a line says yes, that doctor will likely send you to the local medical commission, and the wait for a visit there can run to several months. So, before the certificato anamnestico is written, work with your medico di base, and with a specialist if needed, to make certain that a yes is necessary. A condition that is cured, under control or mild may not call for one. That takes time, but far less than a referral to the commission. Read the certificato anamnestico before you hand it in, and go back to your medico di base about any yes you did not expect.

## Through a driving school

## As a private candidate

- Enroll. Choose an authorized driving school and sign up, bringing the certificato anamnestico from step 3. The school tells you which other documents to bring, and it usually takes your photo for the application. Choose it with the questions in step 2.

- Visit the Ministry-authorized doctor. This doctor issues the medical certificate (certificato medico) that goes into your application. The school books the visit for you with a Ministry-authorized doctor, usually on its own premises. Bring the certificato anamnestico, because the Ministry-authorized doctor cannot issue the medical certificate without it. The doctor checks your eyesight, reads the certificato anamnestico, and issues the medical certificate.

If the certificato anamnestico has a yes. The Ministry-authorized doctor decides whether to issue the medical certificate or to send you to the local medical commission (Commissione Medica Locale). With a yes on the certificato anamnestico, the doctor will likely send you to the commission. The commission can still approve you, but it adds months. It judges epilepsy unless a neurologist certifies it cured, past dependence on alcohol, regular use of medicines that affect driving, serious psychiatric or neurological conditions, kidney failure that needs dialysis or a transplant, and physical impairments. The rules allow the Ministry-authorized doctor to certify a heart condition on a cardiologist's report from a public facility, and diabetes on a diabetologist's report, but practice varies from one doctor to the next, so do not count on it. Any other doubt the doctor has about your fitness to drive also sends you to the commission. If the certificato anamnestico does carry a yes, bring a recent report from your specialist to the visit. The commission will want it.

- The school files your application. It submits the TT2112 form to your provincial Motorizzazione office with your documents, the medical certificate and the fee receipts. The medical certificate must be under 3 months old when the application is filed. From the day of filing you have 6 months and 2 attempts to pass the theory exam. At least a month must pass between a failed attempt and the next.

Pass the theory exam. It has 30 true/false questions in 20 minutes, and you may get no more than 3 wrong. It is offered in Italian, with French and German versions, and never in English. No interpreter or dictionary is allowed in the room. It is held in computerized rooms at the Motorizzazione, not at the school. This is what this app prepares you for.

The school decides when to book your theory exam. Most schools wait until you have a good average on the mock exams in their own app, and your work in this app is not visible to them. So plan on taking the school's mock exams too once your study is done, unless the school accepts your results here (the question in step 2). This is the school's own condition, not a Ministry rule. Ask early what average it wants, over how many mock exams, and how far ahead the exam dates are booked.

On the day. This is what candidates commonly meet, though offices differ. Your phone, switched off, goes into a locker with your coat and hat. Nothing comes into the room with you. A camera checks your face at the entrance and again at your desk, against the photo on your application. The staff explain the procedure in Italian only. Before the exam starts, the screen asks you to accept two statements, so ask your school beforehand what they say. You must accept them to begin. The exam screen has the same layout as the mock exam in this app, and you can go back over your answers before ending it. The result may not appear on the screen at the end, and it can reach you later through the school.

- Get your foglio rosa and take the mandatory driving lessons. Passing the theory exam earns the foglio rosa, a learner permit valid 12 months. You must complete 6 hours of certified driving lessons at the school. The 6 hours are three blocks of 2 hours. One block is on the autostrada or a main extra-urban road, one on a secondary extra-urban road or an urban arterial road, and one at night. You may take at most 2 hours of driving lessons a day. The driving test can be booked only once all 6 hours are certified. A decree of November 2025 raises the 6 hours to 8, in four modules, for a foglio rosa issued once the decree takes effect, so ask your school which rule applies to you. After the school certifies the driving lessons, you may practice with a companion who sits beside you. The companion must be no older than 65 and must have held a B license, or a higher category, for at least 10 years. The car must display a "P" plate.

- Pass the driving test. The school books the driving test and provides the car, which must have dual controls. You may take the test from 1 month after the foglio rosa is issued. You have up to 3 attempts within its 12 months, with at least a month between a failed attempt and the next. It may be held in a different town from the theory exam, one of several in the province, so ask the school where yours will be. A candidate from outside the EU must show a valid residence permit (permesso di soggiorno), or the receipt for its application or renewal, at the test. A Motorizzazione examiner checks that you prepare the car for safe driving, watches two maneuvers, one of them in reverse, and rides with you for at least 25 minutes in normal traffic. You may take the test in an automatic, but the license then carries code 78 and is valid only for automatics.

- Receive your license. A B license covers cars and vans up to 3.5 t with up to 9 seats, a light trailer up to 750 kg, and, in Italy only, motorcycles up to 125 cc and 11 kW. It is valid 10 years until age 50, then 5 years to 70, 3 years to 80, and 2 years after that. As a new driver (neopatentato) you have extra limits for the first three years, such as a cap on vehicle power and a stricter alcohol limit.

Cost. There is no single fee. The certificato anamnestico costs about €50, and the medical certificate carries a €16 revenue stamp (marca da bollo) on top of the Ministry-authorized doctor's fee. The school's enrollment and paperwork fee usually runs €150 to €300, more in big cities, and it normally includes the Ministry fees. Driving lessons are usually sold in 30-minute units of about €30 each, less in packages. The 6 mandatory hours therefore come to roughly €300 to €400. All in, getting the license through a school typically costs €800 to €1,500. When an exam is booked, the school usually asks for a small deposit, which you lose if you miss the exam without a valid reason. A driving lesson usually has to be cancelled 24 hours ahead.

- Visit the Ministry-authorized doctor. This doctor issues the medical certificate (certificato medico) that goes into your application. You book the visit yourself with a Ministry-authorized doctor. You can go to the ASL medical office, booked through the CUP, or to a Ministry-authorized doctor who sees candidates at a driving school or at a vehicle-paperwork agency (agenzia di pratiche auto). Bring the certificato anamnestico, because the Ministry-authorized doctor cannot issue the medical certificate without it. The doctor checks your eyesight, reads the certificato anamnestico, and issues the medical certificate. The medical certificate must be under 3 months old when you file your application.

If the certificato anamnestico has a yes. The Ministry-authorized doctor decides whether to issue the medical certificate or to send you to the local medical commission (Commissione Medica Locale). With a yes on the certificato anamnestico, the doctor will likely send you to the commission. The commission can still approve you, but it adds months. It judges epilepsy unless a neurologist certifies it cured, past dependence on alcohol, regular use of medicines that affect driving, serious psychiatric or neurological conditions, kidney failure that needs dialysis or a transplant, and physical impairments. The rules allow the Ministry-authorized doctor to certify a heart condition on a cardiologist's report from a public facility, and diabetes on a diabetologist's report, but practice varies from one doctor to the next, so do not count on it. Any other doubt the doctor has about your fitness to drive also sends you to the commission. If the certificato anamnestico does carry a yes, bring a recent report from your specialist to the visit. The commission will want it.

- Pay the fees. Pay on pagoPA, through the Portale dell'Automobilista, in your own name. The application is tariff N067, €26.40 plus €16 stamp duty, €42.40 in all. Keep the receipt with its IUV code. The driving test later needs tariff N019, €16.

- File your application. Complete and sign the TT2112 form and submit it to your provincial Motorizzazione office. Add a copy of a valid ID, front and back, your codice fiscale or health card, front and back, the medical certificate, the pagoPA receipt and a 33 × 40 mm photo (some offices ask for two). A non-EU citizen also adds a copy of the residence permit. Some offices take the application at the counter and others by email as a single PDF, so ask your office first. The office then tells you the date of your theory exam. From the day of filing you have 6 months and 2 attempts to pass it. At least a month must pass between a failed attempt and the next.

Pass the theory exam. It has 30 true/false questions in 20 minutes, and you may get no more than 3 wrong. It is offered in Italian, with French and German versions, and never in English. No interpreter or dictionary is allowed in the room. It is held in computerized rooms at the Motorizzazione. This is what this app prepares you for.

On the day. This is what candidates commonly meet, though offices differ. Your phone, switched off, goes into a locker with your coat and hat. Nothing comes into the room with you. A camera checks your face at the entrance and again at your desk, against the photo on your application. The staff explain the procedure in Italian only. Before the exam starts, the screen asks you to accept two statements, so find out beforehand what they say. You must accept them to begin. The exam screen has the same layout as the mock exam in this app, and you can go back over your answers before ending it. The result may not appear on the screen at the end, and it can reach you later.

- Get your foglio rosa and take the mandatory driving lessons. Passing the theory exam earns the foglio rosa, a learner permit valid 12 months. This is where a private candidate needs a driving school. The 6 hours of mandatory driving lessons must be taken at an authorized school, in the three blocks listed for the school route. The driving test cannot be booked until the school certifies the 6 hours. Choose a school now, with the questions in step 2. You will most likely take the driving test in its car too (next step). After the school certifies the driving lessons, you may practice with a companion who sits beside you. The companion must be no older than 65 and must have held a B license, or a higher category, for at least 10 years. The car must display a "P" plate.

- Pass the driving test. The test car must have dual controls, so in practice you take the driving test in a driving school's car. Some offices require a private candidate to go through a school for this step. You may take the test from 1 month after the foglio rosa is issued. You have up to 3 attempts within its 12 months, with at least a month between a failed attempt and the next. It may be held in a different town from the theory exam, one of several in the province, so ask the school where yours will be. A candidate from outside the EU must show a valid residence permit (permesso di soggiorno), or the receipt for its application or renewal, at the test. Bring the same TT2112 file, now with your foglio rosa, your driving-lesson record and the N019 receipt. The test itself is the same as on the school route.

- Receive your license. A B license covers cars and vans up to 3.5 t with up to 9 seats, a light trailer up to 750 kg, and, in Italy only, motorcycles up to 125 cc and 11 kW. It is valid 10 years until age 50, then 5 years to 70, 3 years to 80, and 2 years after that. As a new driver (neopatentato) you have extra limits for the first three years, such as a cap on vehicle power and a stricter alcohol limit.

Cost. This is the cheapest route and the most work. The certificato anamnestico costs about €50, and the Ministry-authorized doctor charges a fee, plus a €16 revenue stamp (marca da bollo) on the medical certificate. The Ministry fees are €42.40 for the application and €16 for the driving test. The 6 mandatory hours of driving lessons come to roughly €300 to €400. The school also charges for the use of its car at the driving test.

How long does it take? Usually 3 to 6 months from start to finish. It can be as little as about 2 months if you study fast and exam dates are available. It can run to about 18 months if you use the full legal periods, 6 months for the theory exam and 12 months of the foglio rosa.

Already have a non-EU license (e.g. US)? It is valid in Italy only for your first 12 months of residency, and you must carry an International Driving Permit or a sworn Italian translation with it. There is no conversion agreement with the US and no exemption for experience. You still take the full theory exam, the driving test and the mandatory driving lessons. Once the 12 months have passed, your foreign license no longer lets you drive in Italy at all. If you are stopped driving on it, you pay a fine and the police take the license on the spot and send it to the Motorizzazione (art. 135 of the Code). If that license has also expired, you are treated as driving without a license, a far heavier offense, and the vehicle is impounded for three months. After the 12 months you may drive only with a foglio rosa, only after a school has certified your mandatory driving lessons, and only with a qualified companion beside you. That is why many people start the process early in their first year.

Wherever you can, look for an English-speaking autoscuola or instructor, and start early in your first 12 months, while your foreign license still lets you drive.

Procedures vary by region and by provincial office, and they change over time. What is described here is the common case, not a rule for every office.
