# About this app and its author

I'm a retired American university professor who recently moved to Italy. Like every American with residency here, I have to pass the Italian written test to get a license. This is no simple task. The written test is not offered in English, which is hard if your Italian reading skills are not that great. Even worse, the questions are worded so that even native Italians struggle to understand them, and in 2025 about [four in ten attempts at the test failed](https://www.mit.gov.it/nfsmitgov/files/media/documentazione/2026-04/Esiti%20Esami%202025.pdf).

If you are here, you're probably like me, an American who already has a driver's license, knows how to drive, and needs to short-circuit the process. I looked at many of the study apps out there, and none of them were written for us. They were English translations of apps made for Italian learners, not apps for American English speakers who are already experienced drivers in the US. Our challenge is not just learning the Italian driving rules and regulations but also learning the Italian vocabulary and grammar used in the exam. As a teacher for over 30 years, I know that the best way to learn something is to teach it. So, I decided to build my own app, using the latest AI tools for writing software, because building it meant working through every one of the over 7000 questions and the rule behind it. The AI tools wrote the software, but I devoted considerable effort to the pedagogical design and the user interface, curating the process very closely. That's why I designed the app to do two things equally well. It teaches the road rules, and it teaches the Italian vocabulary and grammar you need to read the questions. On the test, an Italian word or phrase you misread or don't understand costs you just as much as a rule you never learned. I'm sharing it with others who find themselves in the same situation.

The Ministry publishes its entire bank of test questions, and the app has all of them, each with an English translation and an explanation of the rule behind it. Those translations and explanations were drafted with AI help, and I'm still checking them topic by topic, so if something looks wrong to you, please let me know with the Report a problem button at the bottom of the app.

I have no intention of selling the app or collecting personal information about you. It is free and has no ads. This is my contribution to the community of Americans living in Italy.
